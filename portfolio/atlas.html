Atlas ATS

Transforming a legacy applicant tracking system into a modern, collaborative hiring engine — built for the pace and stakes of executive search.

Overview

Atlas is a recruitment platform that connects fast-growing companies with top executive talent. Internally, they relied on a custom-built Applicant Tracking System — but it was holding them back.

The system had outdated, manual workflows, a cluttered and unintuitive UI, and weak collaboration tools. Engaged as a freelance designer on retainer, I led the UX/UI redesign of the Atlas ATS — transforming it into a modern, intuitive platform focused on clarity, collaboration, and recruiter productivity.

Challenge

A legacy system holding the team back

The legacy ATS was reactive and inconsistent. Recruiters were doing their actual job — finding and placing executive talent — around the tool rather than with it.

✕ Before — Key challenges
  • Recruiters relied on spreadsheets, email threads, and disconnected tools to fill in what the ATS couldn't do.
  • Candidate pipelines were hard to track or manage, with no clear sense of stage or ownership.
  • Collaboration between recruiters and account managers was inefficient — context lived in heads and inboxes, not the system.
Goal

A hiring engine, not just a tracker

The redesign had to do four things — together, not in isolation:

✓ Goals for the redesign
  • Centralize the end-to-end hiring workflow in one place.
  • Improve collaboration across recruiting teams and with clients.
  • Streamline sourcing, candidate tracking, and client handoff.
  • Increase recruiter speed and overall system usability.
Design Process

A lean, sprint-based approach

We followed a lean, sprint-based approach to deliver fast iterations while staying closely aligned with client feedback. Each phase fed directly into the next, with early artifacts surfacing decisions before pixels were ever pushed.

01

Kickoff & Alignment

Gathered goals, challenges, and must-haves directly with the client. This was where we surfaced the gap between what the legacy system did and what recruiters actually needed day-to-day.

02

Competitive Benchmarking

Studied existing ATS platforms to map what users already expected — and where there was room to do something smarter. Used these patterns as both reference and contrast.

03

Low-Fidelity Wireframes

Sketched core screens — Add Job, List View, Candidate Cards — to establish structure and test flows before committing to visual decisions. Wireframes were also the fastest way to collect early client feedback and adjust direction.

Results

What the redesign actually changed

Less task-switching
Visibility
Improved visibility across the hiring workflow meant recruiters spent less time hunting for information across tools.
Better collaboration
Cross-functional
Increased cross-functional team collaboration — especially with clients — as context lived in the system, not in inboxes.
Clearer ownership
Early tester feedback
Testers reported clearer ownership, faster updates, and less manual tracking across the candidate pipeline.
Ready to scale
Growth-ready platform
Positioned Atlas to scale alongside the business — the system can hold more recruiters, clients, and complexity without breaking.
Conclusion

The redesign transformed Atlas from a legacy tool into a collaborative hiring engine. By focusing on clarity, task efficiency, and recruiter collaboration, the system now supports the fast pace and high stakes of executive search — instead of slowing it down.

Next Phase · In progress

A visual and functional upgrade — without losing the recruiter-first feel

We're now working on a major visual and functional upgrade to elevate the platform's UI and brand identity, while keeping accessibility and clarity at the core for recruiters.

The goal is to scale the platform without sacrificing speed or usability — so Atlas remains a high-performing, recruiter-first system as the business grows.

Key areas of focus
  • A redesigned "Add Job" flow for faster, more flexible job setup
  • Streamlined automation setup to reduce friction in task configuration
  • Exploration of AI-powered enhancements — smart candidate suggestions, automated screening prompts, and next-step nudges
